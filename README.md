## About This Repository

Welcome to my Machine Learning portfolio!

This repository brings together my academic coursework, programming assignments, technical reports, and practical projects developed throughout my undergraduate and master's studies, alongside additional professional training in Machine Learning and Deep Learning.

With a background in **Mathematics and Computer Science**, I am particularly interested in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, deep neural networks, computer vision, and the design and implementation of intelligent systems.

The portfolio is organized into three main sections:

- **Babeș-Bolyai University (UBB):** Undergraduate coursework in Artificial Intelligence and AI-based Computer Vision for Automated Driving.
- **University of Copenhagen (UCPH):** Graduate-level coursework in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and related topics.
- **IT School:** Practical Machine Learning and Deep Learning training, including three applied projects.

My goal is to combine mathematical understanding with practical software development and machine learning experimentation.
